Manifesto — Human, Society, State

For Georgia’s national interests, democratic development and public welfare.

We, the members of the National Platform of Georgian Civil Society Organizations, believe that the success, security and development of the country are based on the joint responsibility of society, the state and partners.

Our purpose is not to serve any political force, ideology or group interest, but to support Georgia’s national interests, citizens’ welfare and democratic development.

Our Principles

1. Georgia and its citizens first

Our main reference point is Georgia’s national interests, strengthening statehood, public welfare and dignified life for every citizen.

2. Strong state and strong society

A strong state and strong civil society are natural partners. A strong society strengthens the state, while a democratic state creates space for citizens’ free and dignified development.

3. Democracy as the foundation of development

Democracy, rule of law, accountability and citizen participation are essential foundations of stability and progress.

4. Respect for human dignity

Every person deserves equal opportunities, dignified treatment and protection of their rights.

5. Dialogue instead of confrontation

We support reducing polarization and building cooperation around common interests through respectful dialogue.

6. Responsible civil society

Civil society must be independent, professional, transparent and accountable to society.

7. Progress, education and innovation

Georgia’s development requires education, knowledge, science, technology and innovation.

8. Strengthening the regions

Georgia’s development must be based on equal opportunities and participation of all regions.

9. Partnership based on equality

Partnership must be based on mutual respect, equality, fairness and shared responsibility.

10. Responsibility for the future

Today’s decisions shape the opportunities of future generations. Our responsibility is to build a safe, democratic, fair and successful Georgia.

Our Shared Commitment

  • Act in accordance with Georgia’s national interests.
  • Support democratic development.
  • Strengthen public trust toward the civil sector.
  • Work with high standards of professionalism and ethics.
  • Promote public unity, progress and citizen-oriented action.

GNPCSO

The National Platform of Georgian Civil Society Organizations is a space for a common goal, dialogue and care of citizens.
