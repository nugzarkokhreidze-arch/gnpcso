Values and Principles

The ethical and professional basis of the Platform’s work.

The Platform’s values define not only what we do, but also how we do it. Our work is based on ethics, responsibility, professionalism and respect for public interest.

Respect for National Interests

We act in support of Georgia’s national interests, public welfare and sustainable development.

Human Dignity

At the center of our work stands the person: rights, freedom, safety and equal opportunities.

Democracy and Rule of Law

We support democratic institutions, rule of law, accountability and citizen participation.

Public Responsibility

Our work serves public interest, not personal or group benefit.

Professionalism

We act based on competence, evidence, facts and high professional standards.

Transparency and Accountability

We ensure openness in financial, programmatic and governance processes.

Equality and Diversity

We respect different experiences, views, identities and opinions.

Dialogue and Cooperation

We see different opinions as an opportunity for development and dialogue as a path to progress.

Independence and Impartiality

We maintain organizational independence and do not submit to improper political, financial or other pressure.

GNPCSO

The National Platform of Georgian Civil Society Organizations is a space for a common goal, dialogue and care of citizens.
