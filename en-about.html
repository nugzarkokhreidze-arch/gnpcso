About Us

An independent, non-partisan and membership-based platform for civil society organizations in Georgia.

About the Platform

The National Platform of Georgian Civil Society Organizations is an independent, non-partisan, non-commercial and membership-based national association. It brings together public organizations to support Georgia’s national interests, democratic development, public welfare and sustainable progress.

The Platform is based on the belief that a strong state and a strong civil society are not competitors. They are natural partners whose cooperation is essential for a stable, fair and democratic future.

Human, Society, State

Human

Dignity, rights, freedom, safety and equal opportunities are at the center of the Platform’s work.

Society

Active, responsible and engaged citizens and organizations are the driving force of development.

State

A democratic, accountable and citizen-oriented state is a shared responsibility and a necessary foundation for progress.

What the Platform Represents

  • Common Goal: civil society organizations unite around tasks that matter for the country.
  • Space for Dialogue: a respectful environment for discussion, cooperation and joint action.
  • Care of Citizens: a public effort focused on citizen welfare, rights and participation.

Why the Platform Exists

The Platform was created to strengthen cooperation, coordinate common action, formulate joint positions, support regional organizations, increase public trust and ensure meaningful civil society participation in the democratic development of Georgia.
