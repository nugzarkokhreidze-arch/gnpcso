Governance

Open, horizontal and accountable management based on participation, rotation and responsibility.

The Platform is governed through a democratic, open, horizontal and accountable governance model. Governance is based on equal participation of members, transparent decision-making, consensus-based cooperation, rotation, prevention of conflicts of interest and regular accountability.

Coordination Council

The Platform has a five-member Coordination Council. It consists of the Chairperson of the Platform and four representatives of member organizations.

The Council coordinates strategic directions, maintains communication with members, prepares decisions, supports working groups and represents the interests of the Platform publicly.

Chairperson of the Platform

The Chairperson is selected through an open call. The Nomination Commission reviews applications, conducts interviews and presents three candidates to the General Assembly, which elects the Chairperson.

Rotational Selection of Four Council Members

The remaining four seats are filled according to the alphabetical order of member organizations for a two-year term. If an organization refuses the seat, the next organization on the list takes its place. The alphabetical list is updated every six months.

Decision-Making

The Council works on the basis of consensus. If consensus is not reached, decisions are made by simple majority.

Activity Assessment

Every year on December 20, the Ethics and Mediation Committee determines the activity coefficient of Council members. Each Council member must demonstrate at least 80% activity.

  • Attendance at Council meetings.
  • Maximum three justified absences with prior notice to the Chairperson.
  • At least one initiative per year.
  • Active public representation of the Platform and its interests.
  • Additional criteria may be defined by the Ethics and Mediation Committee.

Nomination Commission

The Nomination Commission is a temporary independent selection and assessment body created for each specific process. It consists of five people selected through an open competition. Members must be independent and free from conflicts of interest.

Committees and Boards

The Platform has the Ethics and Mediation Committee, Audit and Transparency Committee, and Appeals Board. Each body may include up to three members elected from Platform members through direct election at the General Assembly.
