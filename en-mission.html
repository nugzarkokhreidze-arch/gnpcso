Mission, Goal and Tasks

The strategic basis of the Platform: stronger civil society organizations, citizen participation and democratic development.

Mission

The mission of the National Platform of Georgian Civil Society Organizations is to strengthen civil society organizations, support national interests, promote democratic development and increase public welfare.

The Platform creates a space for a common goal, dialogue and care of citizens, where organizations cooperate for the development of the country.

Goal

The main goal of the Platform is to unite civil society organizations around shared values, public responsibility and democratic development, so that civil society becomes an active participant in the country’s progress, protection of national interests and democratic transformation.

Main Objectives

  • Support national interests.
  • Strengthen democratic institutions.
  • Develop civil society organizations.
  • Promote citizen participation.
  • Develop public dialogue.
  • Strengthen regional organizations.
  • Support partnership-based cooperation.
  • Support human rights and the rule of law.
  • Promote citizen-oriented public initiatives.

Tasks

The Platform implements its goals through the following tasks:

  • Strengthen cooperation and coordination among civil society organizations.
  • Prepare joint positions, policy documents and recommendations.
  • Organize consultations on issues of public importance.
  • Promote public awareness and citizen participation.
  • Strengthen the involvement and capacities of regional organizations.
  • Cooperate with state institutions, academia, private sector and international partners.
  • Develop an ethical, transparent and accountable civil sector.
  • Strengthen trust, dialogue and cooperation culture in society.
  • Promote citizen-oriented approaches in public and institutional processes.
  • Support sharing of education, knowledge, innovation and best practices.
  • Increase capacities of member organizations and develop thematic working groups.
  • Strengthen public communication on nationally important issues.
