Membership

Open membership based on shared values, responsibility and citizen-oriented work.

Who Can Become a Member

Membership is open to public organizations registered in Georgia that share the Platform’s goals and values, recognize its founding documents, agree with ethical standards and are ready to participate in Platform activities.

Two-Year Membership Renewal Model

Every two years, the full membership of the Platform is renewed. The Nomination Commission assesses organizations wishing to continue their membership mandate and, if necessary, conducts interviews.

Based on the assessment, the Commission forms a composition of approximately 60–80 organizations and grants them appropriate status.

Assessment Criteria

  • Regional representation.
  • Gender balance and inclusiveness.
  • Areas of organizational activity.
  • Compliance with the Platform’s founding documents.
  • Respect for ethical standards.
  • Quality of public work and effectiveness for the Platform.

Membership Statuses

Full Member

An organization that fully participates in the Platform’s work and decision-making.

Associate Member

An organization that shares the Platform’s values and participates with defined powers.

Partner Organization

An organization cooperating with the Platform in specific areas.

Honorary Member or Supporter

A person or organization with a significant contribution to civil society or democratic development.

Rights and Responsibilities

  • Participate in Platform activities and working groups.
  • Receive information about decisions and activities.
  • Submit initiatives and contribute to joint positions.
  • Respect the founding documents, ethical standards and other members.
  • Act transparently, responsibly and in good faith.
