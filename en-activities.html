Activities

Coordination, dialogue, recommendations, regional cooperation and citizen-oriented initiatives.

The Platform’s activities are directed toward coordination of civil society organizations, public dialogue, democratic development, citizen participation, regional strengthening and support for national interests.

Public Dialogue

Dialogue among civil society organizations, state institutions, academia, business, media and international partners.

Policy Documents

Preparation of policy papers, analyses, recommendations and initiatives on issues of public importance.

Statements and Positions

Public positions on democracy, human rights, rule of law, national interests and public matters.

Regional Cooperation

Strengthening regional organizations and presenting regional needs at the national level.

Capacity Development

Sharing knowledge, experience and best practices; strengthening organizational capacities.

Citizen-Oriented Initiatives

Initiatives that support citizen rights, participation, welfare, awareness and engagement.

GNPCSO

The National Platform of Georgian Civil Society Organizations is a space for a common goal, dialogue and care of citizens.
